Political Economy of Technology · Economics of Innovation · Public Policy

I am Boyan Zou (邹博延), a third-year undergraduate student at the University of Toronto, double-majoring in Economics and Public Policy.

My research interests lie primarily in the political economy of technology, with a particular focus on how technological change interacts with firms, markets, institutions, and public policy. I am interested in understanding the economic and institutional forces shaping technological innovation, as well as its implications for corporate behaviour, resource allocation, and public governance.

My research draws on empirical, theoretical, and computational methods to explore questions at the intersection of economics, technology, and public policy.
